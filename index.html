First aquarium

How a first tank comes together.

The fish are the last step. First, the water. We will help you get the filter and the setup right before you add anything.

Plan your first tank
  1. 01

    Tell us what you have in mind

    A tank size, a budget, or a photo of the corner where it will go. We will start there.

  2. 02

    Get the water and filter right

    We talk through filters, water, plants and how long to wait before fish go in.

  3. 03

    Choose fish that fit

    Goldfish, community fish or a species that suits your tank. We will be honest about what works.

  4. 04

    Set it up, then ask questions

    Take home the tank and the basics. Message us if the water clouds or something does not look right.

Take your time. Ask as many questions as you need.

Ask the shop
What can I find at Pets Magic?

Fish and aquarium setups, birds and cages, dog and cat food, treats, toys, leashes, litter boxes and everyday supplies.

Can you help me set up my first aquarium?

Yes. We will talk through tank size, filters, water and the fish that suit your experience. A good tank takes a little patience to set up.

Do you groom dogs and cats?

Yes, for dogs and cats, by appointment. Call the grooming line on +91 91562 96060 or message us on WhatsApp.

Do you sell birds and cages?

Birds and cages are part of the shop, including lovebirds, cockatiels and finches when available.

Can you deliver?

Send your location and what you need on WhatsApp. We will confirm whether delivery is possible, the coverage and any charges.

Can I check if a product is in stock before I come?

Send the product name on WhatsApp and we will check the shelves. If we do not have it, we will tell you.

Where exactly is the shop?

We are on the Aundh-Ravet BRT road in Tathawade, near Maruti Temple. The full address and map are in the visit section below.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 10am to 9:30pm. Saturday and Sunday, 10am to 10pm.
